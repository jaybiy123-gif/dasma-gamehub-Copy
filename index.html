THE STORY BEHIND THE HUB

DASMA GAMEHUB

KEEPING THE PAST ALIVE. CONNECTING GENERATIONS THROUGH GAMING.

We are one of the unsung heroes of the gaming industry.

At DASMA GAMEHUB, we believe that gaming is more than just technology, graphics, or the latest console releases. It is about memories, family bonding, friendships, and the unforgettable experiences that games bring into our lives.

While the gaming industry continues to move forward, we believe the classics deserve to move forward with it.

We stand for the preservation of gaming history.

We support older gaming consoles by repairing and restoring original controllers, helping breathe new life into the hardware that once brought families and friends together. We believe these classic machines still deserve to be played, enjoyed, and appreciated.

But our mission goes beyond repairs.

We are also advocates of game preservation. Every classic game carries a piece of gaming history — a creative vision, a memorable adventure, and an experience worth sharing. We want the next generation to discover these treasures, understand where gaming began, and experience the same joy that earlier generations grew up with.

We believe physical games, original hardware, and authentic gaming experiences deserve a place in the future.

OUR BELIEFS

WHAT WE STAND FOR

🎮

Console Preservation

Keeping classic gaming hardware alive and giving old consoles another chance to entertain.

🔧

Controller Restoration

Supporting original controllers and restoring the experience they were designed to deliver.

💿

Game Preservation

Keeping physical games accessible so their stories, creativity, and history can be enjoyed by future generations.

❤️

Generations of Gaming

Helping parents share the games they grew up with and creating new memories with their children.

OUR PROMISE

SMALL EFFORTS. A LASTING LEGACY.

We may not be the biggest name in the gaming industry, but we believe even the smallest efforts can make a difference in preserving something meaningful.

Every controller restored, every classic game kept in circulation, and every old console brought back to life is another small victory for gaming history.

Because the games we grew up with should not simply become forgotten memories.

They deserve to be played again.
